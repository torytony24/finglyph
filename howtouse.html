Finglyph

How to use Behind Description

Finglyph is a sign language typography editor that turns hand gestures into visual art instead of text. By drawing and erasing letters in front of the camera, both deaf and hearing users can experience the beauty of manual alphabets recording and sharing their initials, words, and sentences.

For accurate recognition, please maintain a distance of about 30 cm from the camera and hold your hand sign steady for a second. Once your gesture is successfully detected, the manual alphabet graphic will clearly appear on the screen. Combine these graphics to freely craft your own initials, words, or full sentences, creating your very own typography piece.

Tap the download icon to save the letters created by your hands. It can be used as a stylish initial postcard for someone special, or a secret encrypted message shared between those who understand the beauty of sign language.

Supports up to 100 characters. Characters beyond the frame will not be shown, so try crafting a concise message within the limit.
